    Opportunity thesis

    Where this app can stand apart

    History-in-context programming

    Coach updates next week while seeing the prior week, readiness, notes, and progressions in one view.

    Coach-owned methodology

    Custom metrics, progression rules, templates, and language adapt to the coach instead of forcing one training philosophy.

    AI as an assistant, not the coach

    Format imports, summarize inputs, search history, and draft suggestions with approval gates.

    Scales from 1:1 to teams

    Same data model supports individual coaching, semi-private groups, sport teams, and multi-coach staff.

    Competitor strengths

    • Bridge Athletic: team performance, robust parameters, AI imports, enterprise strength rooms.
    • TrainHeroic: athlete experience, marketplace, training calendar, video review, chat.
    • Kahunas: affordable all-in-one coaching, workouts, diet, supplements, check-ins.
    • Everfit and HubFit: polished client app, habits, messaging, nutrition, automations.

    Pain points to attack

    • Too much clicking to update programs after reviewing athlete data.
    • Previous set history hidden away from the active logging moment.
    • Rigid streaks and generic reminders that create shame instead of adaptation.
    • Data spread across workouts, food, media, chat, and check-ins without a coach decision layer.
